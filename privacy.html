Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026 · HotScan India · hotscan.in

1. Who We Are

HotScan India ("HotScan", "we", "our") is a web application operated by Mahak Fahad, Chennai, India. We help Hot Wheels collectors identify cars, check live Indian market prices, and manage their collections.

2. What Data We Collect

Account information: When you sign in with Google or email, we collect your email address and a display name. We do not access any other Google account data.

Collection data: Cars you add to your collection — name, rarity, price data, and optional thumbnail image — are stored in our database and synced to your account.

Scan images: Photos you upload for scanning are sent to Groq AI for identification. We compress images to ≤512px before sending. Images are not stored after the scan is complete.

Payment information: If you subscribe to Pro, payment is processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card number. We receive only a payment confirmation from Razorpay.

Usage data: We use Plausible Analytics — a privacy-first, cookieless analytics tool — to understand how many people use the app. No personal data is collected by analytics.

Error data: We use Sentry to log JavaScript errors. Error reports include the browser type, OS, and the code line that failed. No personal data is included in error reports.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.
  • We do not run ads or work with ad networks.
  • We do not use tracking cookies.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your device contacts, camera roll, or microphone.

4. How We Store Data

Your data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS, ap-south-1 / us-east-1). Supabase uses Row Level Security — your data is only accessible to your account. We use HTTPS everywhere.

5. Your Rights

You can request deletion of all your account data at any time by emailing mahakfahad07@gmail.com. We will delete your account and all associated data within 7 days.

You can export your collection data from the app at any time using the PDF export feature.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Groq AI — processes scan images for car identification. Groq Privacy Policy
  • Supabase — database and authentication. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Razorpay — payment processing. Razorpay Privacy Policy
  • Plausible Analytics — cookieless analytics. Plausible Privacy Policy
  • Sentry — error monitoring. Sentry Privacy Policy

7. Children

HotScan is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes

If we make significant changes to this policy, we will notify users via the app. Continued use of HotScan after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions? Email us at mahakfahad07@gmail.com
