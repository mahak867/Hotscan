HotScan India

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Overview

HotScan India ("the App", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account data: When you sign up, we store your email address, username, and profile details in Supabase (our database provider).
  • Collection data: Cars you add to your collection are stored in your local device storage and optionally synced to our Supabase database when you are signed in.
  • Scan images: Photos you upload for scanning are sent to Groq's API for AI processing. We do not permanently store your scan images.
  • Community prices: Prices you submit are stored in our database and displayed to other users.
  • Marketplace listings: Listings you create, including your city and WhatsApp number, are stored in our database and shown to other users.
  • Usage data: We use Plausible Analytics (privacy-friendly, no cookies, no personal data) to understand aggregate app usage.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the core scanning, collection, and marketplace features.
  • To sync your collection across devices when you are signed in.
  • To display community-submitted prices to all users.
  • To process Pro subscription payments via Razorpay.
  • To improve the App based on aggregate usage patterns.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — database and authentication (servers in Singapore/US). Privacy Policy
  • Groq — AI inference for image and text analysis. Images are sent to Groq solely for processing. Privacy Policy
  • Razorpay — payment processing for Pro subscriptions. Privacy Policy
  • Plausible Analytics — cookieless, GDPR-compliant analytics. No personal data is collected. Data Policy

5. Your Groq API Key

If you choose to enter your own Groq API key, it is stored only in your device's local storage. It is never sent to our servers — only to Groq's API directly from your device.

6. Data Retention

Account and collection data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data by contacting us at mahakfahad07@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Security

We use Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure users can only access their own data. All data is transmitted over HTTPS.

9. Your Rights

Under applicable laws (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at mahakfahad07@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy. We will notify you of significant changes via the App.

11. Contact

Questions? Email us at mahakfahad07@gmail.com
