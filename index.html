🔍 Condition Grading Guide
Condition affects value significantly. Use this guide when buying or selling.
MOC
Mint on Card
Sealed in original blister. No bends, scratches or fading. Perfect card and car.
100% of value
Factory sealed blister with intact hang-tab. Spectraflame paint must be pristine. TH/STH in MOC can hold value for decades. Always store in a UV-protective case.
NM
Near Mint
Tiny imperfections. Card may have minor shelf wear. Car is perfect.
80-90% of value
Opened but never played with. Common for loose collectors. Card may have a price sticker or corner ding. Most loose Real Riders are sold at NM grade.
VG
Very Good
Visible wear on card. Car may have light play wear. Still displayable.
50-70% of value
Inspect under light — small paint chips and wheel axle dirt are common. Good for customs or display only. Negotiate hard: "VG" on OLX often hides LP condition.
LP
Loose / Played
Removed from card. Paint chips, wheel wear, scratches visible.
20-40% of value
Best for custom builders or display racers. Wheels often need replacing. STH in LP grade can still fetch ₹800–2000 due to Spectraflame rarity. Always check axle integrity.
