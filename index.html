Spotter's guide

What to check before money changes hands. Use it alongside a scan, not instead of your own eyes.

Treasure Hunt or Super?
Treasure Hunt
  • Low-production flame-in-circle logo printed somewhere on the car
  • Silver or grey circle-flame on the card, behind the car
  • Ordinary enamel paint
  • Ordinary plastic wheels
Super Treasure Hunt
  • Small “TH” tampo on the body
  • Gold circle-flame on the card, behind the car
  • Spectraflame paint: deep, metallic, candy-like
  • Real Riders: rubber tyres you can feel with a fingernail

Wheels or paint alone do not prove a Super. A wheel swap takes five minutes. Confirm the card flame and the TH tampo, and compare with the mainline version of the same casting from that year.

Fake-spotting checklist

Tick what you have checked. Nothing on this list proves a car genuine on its own. Several misses together is your answer.

Buying on Instagram or WhatsApp

Most collector trades in India happen in DMs and groups, paid by UPI. A UPI transfer cannot be reversed the way a card payment can, so the checking has to happen before you pay.

  1. Ask for a timestamp photo. The car next to a handwritten note with today's date and the seller's username. It proves they hold the car right now.
  2. Ask for the base and the card back. Run the base photo through Fake check, and compare the card with the checklist above.
  3. Look at the seller's history. Older posts, tagged buyers, and vouches in a group you both belong to. A new account with stock photos is a no.
  4. Agree the total up front. Price, courier charge, and who pays if it is damaged in transit. Carded cars should ship in a box, not a cover.
  5. Prefer meeting or cash on delivery for costly cars. For a Super or a vintage piece, a public meet-up beats an advance transfer to a stranger.
  6. Record an unboxing video in one take from sealed parcel to car. It is the only evidence either side has if something is wrong.
Condition grades and what they do to price
MOC
Mint on Card
100%
Sealed blister, flat card, intact hang tab, no price-sticker tear.
NM
Near Mint
80–90%
Soft corner or light shelf wear on the card. Car untouched.
VG
Very Good
50–70%
Creased card or opened car with no visible chips.
LP
Loose, played
20–40%
Paint chips, bent axles, worn tampo. Check the axles roll straight.
